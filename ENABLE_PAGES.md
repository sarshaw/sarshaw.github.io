# How to Enable GitHub Pages - Visual Guide

## Current Location
You're currently in: **Settings > Actions**

## Next Steps

### 1. Click "Pages" in Left Sidebar
- Look at the left sidebar
- Under "Code and automation" section
- Click on **"Pages"** (it's below "Runners" and above "Models")

### 2. Configure Pages Settings
Once you're on the Pages settings page:

1. **Source section:**
   - Select: **"Deploy from a branch"** (radio button)

2. **Branch section:**
   - **Branch dropdown:** Select **"main"**
   - **Folder dropdown:** Select **"/ (root)"**

3. Click **"Save"** button

### 3. Wait for Build
- GitHub will automatically start building
- You'll see a message: "Your site is ready to be published at https://sarshaw.github.io"
- Check the **Actions** tab to see build progress
- Wait 1-2 minutes for the build to complete

### 4. Verify Your Site
- Visit: https://sarshaw.github.io
- Your site should be live!

## Quick Navigation
**Settings > Pages** (in left sidebar under "Code and automation")
